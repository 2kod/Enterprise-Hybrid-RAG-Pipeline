# Employee Handbook: Time Off Policies

## Vacation Days
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Vacation days must be requested at least two weeks in advance and approved by the immediate supervisor. Unused vacation days (up to 5 days) can be rolled over to the next calendar year.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. For absences exceeding three consecutive days, a doctor's note may be required.

## Public Holidays
The company observes all federal public holidays. If a holiday falls on a weekend, the company will observe it on the nearest weekday.
